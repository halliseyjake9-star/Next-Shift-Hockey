Three programs

Every program includes the twelve-metric baseline, the February re-score, and the player portal. What changes is how much coaching time sits between them. Nothing here is unlimited — the limits are written down so we can actually deliver what we sold.

Foundation

$149 / month

For younger players and families who mostly need structure.

  • One mentor session a month, 30 minutes
  • One film review a month
  • 8–12 week development plan
  • Weekly plan and Sunday reflection
  • Player portal
  • Quarterly parent review
  • Email support, two business days
Most families start here

Performance

$299 / month

The full system, at the point in a career where it matters most.

  • Two mentor sessions a month, 30 minutes each
  • Two film reviews a month
  • Custom development plan and KPI tracking
  • Weekly plan and Sunday reflection
  • Player profile and resume
  • Pathway research and target list
  • Monthly written parent report
  • Messaging support, one business day

Elite

$599 / month

Capped at 12 players per advisor. When it's full, it's full.

  • Weekly mentor call
  • Weekly film feedback
  • Full season development plan
  • Parent strategy calls, monthly
  • Pathway management and communication strategy
  • Film and profile assistance
  • Event and showcase planning
  • Priority messaging, same business day

Also available on their own: standalone player evaluation, $249. Film breakdown, $129. Pathway strategy session, $199.
