What you actually get

A written evaluation against a fixed twelve-metric rubric, a development plan built from the two or three things holding your player back, weekly work they can measure, and film review tied to the same metrics. Then we score it again and show you the movement.

  1. Baseline

    Three games of film. Twelve metrics, scored one to five against written anchors. Every extreme score carries a note explaining what we saw. Anything we can't see gets marked not scored.

  2. The block

    Eight to twelve weeks against three objectives. Weekly plan every Monday, a reflection every Sunday, film review tied back to the objectives rather than a highlight reel.

  3. Re-score

    Same rubric, same anchors, February. You get the delta and the evidence behind it, whether or not it flatters us.

What we won't tell you

We don't promise roster spots, scholarships, offers, or exposure. Nobody can, and anyone who does is selling you something else. We don't negotiate on your player's behalf or act as an agent. We tell you where your player is, what needs to improve, and what the realistic next step looks like — including when the honest answer is that the level you're aiming at isn't the right one.

Families keep paying for this because the assessment holds up when a prep coach asks a hard question about it.
